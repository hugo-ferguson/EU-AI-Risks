FR-01 - The system shall allow the user to select a travel destination (country, city, or region) from a searchable list or by typing a destination name, with auto-suggestions for matching locations.

FR-02 - The system shall validate the selected destination and prompt the user to correct or reselect it if the destination is not recognized or is ambiguous.

FR-03 - The system shall retrieve relevant, up-to-date safety information for the selected destination from configured data sources, such as government travel advisories, crime statistics, health alerts, and weather or natural hazard reports.

FR-04 - The system shall use an AI model to generate short, clear travel safety tips (e.g., 3 to 7 tips, each no longer than two sentences) based on the retrieved safety information for the selected destination.

FR-05 - The system shall categorize each generated tip by topic (e.g., personal safety, health, transportation, scams, local laws and customs, natural hazards) so users can quickly scan the results.

FR-06 - The system shall display the generated tips to the user in a readable format, along with the destination name, the information sources used, and the date the information was last updated.

FR-07 - The system shall include a disclaimer with the tips stating that they are general guidance and not a substitute for official government travel advisories.

FR-08 - The system shall allow the user to change the selected destination at any time and shall regenerate the tips for the new destination without requiring the user to restart the application.

FR-09 - The system shall handle errors, such as unavailable data sources or AI generation failures, by displaying a clear message to the user and offering to retry or showing the most recent cached tips where available.
