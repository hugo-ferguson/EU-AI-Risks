FR-01 The system shall store, categorize, and manage a library of audio assets (masked or low-volume voice messages, tones, and embedded suggestion tracks), each linked to specific product categories or campaigns.

FR-02 The system shall let authorized operators import and maintain product catalogs with margin data, stock levels, and store locations, and designate high-margin products as promotion targets.

FR-03 The system shall embed target messages into background audio (e.g., in-store music) at levels below conscious perception, by masking, layering, or frequency manipulation.

FR-04 The system shall deliver audio through store speaker infrastructure, with independent control of content, volume, and timing for each store zone or aisle.

FR-05 The system shall use in-store sensors (e.g., occupancy counters, footfall sensors, point-of-sale feeds) to estimate shopper presence, density, and dwell time in each zone.

FR-06 The system shall let operators schedule campaigns by time, day, zone, and shopper traffic conditions, and shall automatically select the audio asset that matches the targeted product and context.

FR-07 The system shall correlate audio playback events with sales data to report conversion uplift, margin impact, and per-campaign performance.

FR-08 The system shall adjust message selection, timing, and frequency based on measured effectiveness and shall support A/B testing of audio variants.

FR-09 The system shall provide a secure operator dashboard with role-based access, remote multi-store management, audit logging of all playback and configuration changes, and system health monitoring.
