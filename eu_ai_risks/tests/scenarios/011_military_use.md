FR-01 - The system shall ingest data from authorized military and intelligence sources, including surveillance feeds, signals intelligence, travel and border records, open-source information, and informant reports, and normalize it into a unified format for analysis.

FR-02 - The system shall resolve and link records that refer to the same individual or entity across multiple data sources, maintaining a consolidated profile for each person of interest.

FR-03 - The system shall analyze each profile using configurable behavioral, relational, and movement-pattern indicators to detect anomalies and activities associated with hostile or foreign-agent behavior.

FR-04 - The system shall assign each individual a risk score and risk category (e.g., low, moderate, high), along with a confidence level and a human-readable explanation of the factors that contributed to the assessment.

FR-05 - The system shall generate real-time alerts to designated analysts and commanders when an individual’s risk score crosses a defined threshold or when a high-priority pattern is detected.

FR-06 - The system shall require human analyst review and authorization before any high-risk designation is finalized or any operational action is recommended, and shall allow analysts to confirm, override, or dismiss AI-generated assessments with recorded justification.

FR-07 - The system shall provide analysts with a dashboard for searching, filtering, and visualizing profiles, relationship networks, geographic activity, and risk trends.

FR-08 - The system shall enforce role-based and clearance-based access control, ensuring users can view only the data and profiles permitted by their authorization level.

FR-09 - The system shall maintain a tamper-proof audit log of all data access, risk assessments, analyst decisions, and system changes to support accountability and oversight.

FR-10 - The system shall support feedback-driven model updates, allowing confirmed outcomes and analyst corrections to be used to retrain and evaluate the models, while monitoring for bias, false positives, and performance degradation.
