FR-01 - The system shall continuously capture live video from cameras installed across designated public squares and process the streams in real time.

FR-02 - The system shall detect all human faces appearing in each video frame and extract a facial feature template for each detected face.

FR-03 - The system shall match each extracted template against a database of known identities and, where no match exists, create a new unique profile for the individual.

FR-04 - The system shall track each identified individual across frames and across multiple camera views, maintaining a continuous movement path within and between monitored areas.

FR-05 - The system shall build and update a profile for each individual from observed data such as visit frequency, dwell time, locations visited, and movement patterns.

FR-06 - The system shall derive commercial attributes for each profile, such as estimated demographics, interests, and behavioral segments, and make them available for marketing and analytics purposes.

FR-07 - The system shall provide an authorized-user dashboard that displays live crowd density, individual tracking, and aggregated and per-profile analytics, with search, filtering, and report export.

FR-08 - The system shall securely store biometric templates, profiles, and tracking history, enforce role-based access control and audit logging, and apply configurable data retention and deletion policies.

FR-09 - The system shall support compliance with applicable privacy and biometric data laws, including public notice signage, consent or opt-out mechanisms where legally required, and individual data access and deletion requests.
