FR-01 - The system shall capture a user’s facial video or image input through the device camera while the user browses adult-content material on the platform.

FR-02 - The system shall detect and extract facial features and expressions (e.g., micro-expressions, gaze direction, pupil dilation, and emotional valence) from the captured input in real time.

FR-03 - The system shall use a trained machine-learning model to map the extracted facial-expression data to inferred sexual preference categories for each user.

FR-04 - The system shall maintain a user preference profile that is updated continuously as new facial-expression data is analyzed across sessions.

FR-05 - The system shall match each user’s inferred preference profile against a catalogue of tagged adult-content items and generate a ranked list of personalized recommendations.

FR-06 - The system shall display the ranked recommendations to the user through the platform interface and refresh them dynamically as the user’s inferred preferences change.

FR-07 - The system shall store captured facial data, inferred preference profiles, and recommendation history in encrypted, access-controlled storage.

FR-08 - The system shall verify that users are adults through an age-verification mechanism before enabling camera capture or any recommendation features.

FR-09 - The system shall present users with an explicit consent request before camera capture begins, and shall allow users to withdraw consent, disable analysis, and delete their stored facial data and inferred profiles at any time.
